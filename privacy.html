Blink Puller privacy policy

Blink Puller is a personal tool used only by its owner.

  • What it accesses: the Google Drive of the person who signs in (the owner), to save and list camera clip files and a few small status files.
  • What it does with it: nothing else. It does not read other files for any other purpose.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared or sent to anyone else.
  • Storage: the sign-in token is kept privately by the owner and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions: use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
